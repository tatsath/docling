## The one strategic truth (don't skip)

Even second-tier bureaus prefer speakers who already (a) command a fee and (b) have demand. So **apply broadly *and* self-book in parallel.** A bureau is far more likely to sign you the week *after* you've been paid for one keynote than the week before. Translation: send these applications, but the email that does the most work this month is still the one converting a Reuters Momentum / NexGen / CQF slot into a *paid* keynote. Bureaus amplify demand; they rarely originate it.

Also: **don't sign an exclusive** early. Most of these are non-exclusive — list with several at once.
