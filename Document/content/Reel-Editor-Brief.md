## Deliverable 1 — The 3-minute speaker reel (16:9, 1080p)

**Structure:**
1. **0:00–0:08 — Cold open.** The single best 8 seconds of you delivering a sharp line on stage (pull from A or B). No intro card first — start on your voice/energy.
2. **0:08–0:14 — Name card.** Lower-third or full card: *"Hariom Tatsat — Financial AI · AI Interpretability · O'Reilly Author."* Keep on screen briefly, then back to footage.
3. **0:14–2:20 — The substance.** Intercut your strongest 20–40 sec stretches from A, B, and C. Prioritize moments where you (a) say something contrarian/memorable, (b) explain a framework, (c) get audience reaction. Show the stage and audience where possible — that signals "people book this person."
4. **Throughout — Logo strip / lower-thirds.** Briefly flash the venue each clip is from: *Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta · AI4 · The AI Summit New York · NVIDIA GTC.*
5. **2:20–2:50 — Build.** Slightly faster cuts of one-line highlights to build energy.
6. **2:50–3:00 — Close card.** *"Hariom Tatsat · Keynotes & Executive Briefings · htatsat.com/speaking"* + email.

**Style notes:** clean, editorial, finance-credible (not flashy YouTuber). Subtle motion only. Light captioning on key lines for sound-off viewing. Color-correct so all three sources look consistent. Royalty-free, understated background music at low level under the talking.

One idea per clip, 45–90 seconds each, burned-in captions, a simple title bar at top. These are for LinkedIn (3 posts/week for a month) and double as proof material.

Pick the five strongest standalone moments across A, B, C — ideally one each on:
1. The trust gap / "most finance AI fails in ways that look right"

**Each clip:** title bar (e.g., *"Why 'human in the loop' isn't enough"*), captions throughout, name handle bottom corner (*@HariomTatsat / htatsat.com*).
