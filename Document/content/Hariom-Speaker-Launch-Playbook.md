## Part 2 — Your signature talk (the spine of the whole kit)

**▶ SEND-READY — Talk title + abstract**

> ### Beyond the Black Box: Can You Trust an AI That Manages Money?
> Banks and asset managers are racing to deploy AI across research, risk, and client workflows — but the dangerous failures are the ones that *look* right. Drawing on published research into the interpretability of large language models in finance, this talk gives leaders a concrete framework for adopting AI in high-stakes financial workflows without blowing up on hidden failure modes: where AI creates real ROI, where it creates fake confidence, and the "risk gates" that make agentic AI safe enough to trust with money.

**Three takeaways (reuse everywhere):**
1. Why output review misses the most dangerous finance-AI errors — and what catches them.
2. The 7 silent failure modes of finance AI (look-ahead leakage, table corruption, citation hallucination, eval blindness, tool-use overreach, data leakage, the demo-to-production gap).
3. The Finance AI Risk-Gate Stack — a 5-layer control model for trustworthy AI in investment workflows.

**Full outline (your deck skeleton):**
1. **Hook:** a confidently wrong investment memo that passed human review. (90 seconds, true-to-life.)
2. **The trust gap:** adoption is everywhere; trust and ROI are not. Why.
3. **Inside the black box:** what interpretability reveals about *why* models fail in finance (your research, made executive-simple).
4. **The 7 silent failure modes** — each with a one-line finance example.
5. **The Risk-Gate Stack** — retrieval → extraction → reasoning/eval → runtime probe → human approval.
6. **The maturity model:** Pilots → Tools → Workflows → Governed → Agentic-with-Gates. "Where is your firm?"
7. **Close:** the AI Trust Index for Financial Institutions — your annual benchmark; the one thing to do Monday.

**Alternate titles for organizers:** "Why Most Finance AI Pilots Fail After the Demo" · "Agentic AI in Finance: Useful, Dangerous, Inevitable" · "From AI Demos to AI-Native Investment Workflows."
