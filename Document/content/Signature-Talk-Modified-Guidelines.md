# Signature Talk — Modified Guidelines
### How to expand your 10-minute "vibe banking" talk into a 40-minute keynote, what to reuse vs. record, and how the new agents paper fits

> This replaces the talk-related guidance in the earlier playbook. Everything else (one-sheet, bios, emails, bureau/podcast lists, 72-hour plan) stays as written.

---

## Decision 1 — Scope: stay finance-anchored (don't broaden the flagship)

**The question:** your new paper is *interpretability of agents* (broader than the previous *interpretability of LLMs in finance*) — should the signature talk go beyond finance?

**The answer: no — not for your flagship.** Here's the logic, because it's the most important call in this whole plan:

- Your premium fee comes from being **the finance-AI-trust person**, not a general AI-interpretability person. In finance-AI you're one of a *few*. In "AI agent interpretability" broadly, you're one of *many* — competing with frontier-lab researchers and academics. Narrow is what commands $15–40K.
- Buyers who pay keynote fees buy a **specific, ownable answer to a specific fear**: "can I trust AI with money / risk / clients?" A general interpretability talk has no buyer with a budget. A finance-trust talk has banks, asset managers, and fintechs with innovation budgets.
- Breadth feels safer to you but reads as *less* authoritative to a booker. The riches are in the niche.

**So:** the new agents paper is a major asset — but use it as **depth *inside* the finance story**, not as a reason to widen the umbrella. It becomes your "frontier research" section: *"I don't just observe these systems — I study how agents fail internally, and here's what that tells you about when a finance agent can be trusted to act."* That's the line no one else on the circuit can say.

**Later, optional:** once the finance flagship is paying, you can spin out a *second* talk for pure-AI audiences ("Beyond the Black Box: Interpreting AI Agents") aimed at AI conferences, dev audiences, and the bigger AI podcasts. Two talks, one menu. But build and monetize the finance one first.

---

## Decision 2 — Reuse vs. record: do both, in this split

| Asset | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Existing Fed / AI4 / NVIDIA / AI Summit footage (the 5–10 min talks) | **Reuse for the reel + clips.** Don't re-record. | This is your *proof* you've performed on marquee stages. Irreplaceable. |
| The expanded 40-min keynote | **Record ONE clean 18–25 min delivery** of the full talk (studio or a webinar you host). | Your current footage shows short slots. One long-form recording proves you can carry a full keynote — and doubles as a webinar/lead magnet. |
| The live keynote at booked events | **Perform it; record each one.** | Each performance produces better reel material than the last. Flywheel. |

So the only *new* recording you need is **one ~20-minute long-form delivery** of the expanded talk. Everything else is reuse. You're not starting from a camera; you're filling one specific gap (proof of long-form).

---

## Decision 3 — What to KEEP from the "vibe banking" talk vs. what to CREATE

Your 10-minute talk is the **spine**. You're going from 10 → ~40 minutes by *adding depth on the same topic*, not by changing topic. Roughly: keep ~10 min of tested material, create ~30 min of expansion.

### KEEP (already audience-tested — don't touch what works)
- **The "vibe banking" hook and title** — it's catchy, contrarian, and *you already know it sells.* Strong candidate for the headline title (or a subtitle).
- The core narrative arc you already deliver.
- Your best finance failure examples / war stories.
- Your delivery style and the lines that got laughs/nods.

### CREATE (the expansion — turns a short slot into a keynote)
1. **The 7 silent failure modes of finance AI** (~6 min) — taxonomy with one finance example each. Makes it feel comprehensive.
2. **The Risk-Gate Stack framework** (~6 min) — retrieval → extraction → reasoning/eval → runtime probe → human approval. Your ownable diagram.
3. **The maturity model** (~4 min) — Pilots → Tools → Workflows → Governed → Agentic-with-Gates. "Where is your firm?" — lets the audience locate themselves.
4. **The frontier-research section** (~4 min) — *here's where the new agents paper goes.* "How agents fail internally and what it means for trusting them with money/risk." This is your unfair-advantage moment.
5. **A second, sharper opening** (~2 min) — a confidently-wrong-AI-output story to set the stakes before the vibe-banking hook.
6. **A strong close + one CTA** (~2 min) — the AI Trust Index idea + "the one thing to do Monday."

### Expanded talk flow (40 min)
Cold-open failure story → vibe-banking hook (kept) → the trust gap → 7 failure modes (new) → Risk-Gate Stack (new) → frontier: how agents fail inside (new, from the paper) → maturity model (new) → close + CTA.

**Modular by design:** this lets you flex length without rewriting —
- **10 min** = hook + 2 failure modes + close (your current talk, basically)
- **20 min** = + Risk-Gate Stack
- **40 min** = the full flow above
- **Half-day workshop** = the full flow + hands-on exercises around each gate

One body of material, four runtimes. That's the leverage.

## Decision 4 — Title (keep flexibility, pick one headline)

Put two on your speaker page; lead with one. Both are the same core material with different hooks:
- **Headline option A (catchy, tested):** *"Does Vibe Banking Actually Work?"* — subtitle: *Can you trust an AI that manages money?*
- **Headline option B (executive-direct):** *"Can You Trust an AI That Manages Money?"* — subtitle: *Beyond the demos: what actually works (and fails) in financial AI.*

Use A for fintech/innovation crowds, B for board/asset-manager crowds. Same talk underneath.

---

## What to do, in order
1. Write the expanded talk **once**: title + abstract + 3 takeaways + ~30-slide deck (keep your tested 10 min, add the 4 new modules).
2. Cut existing footage into the **reel + clips** (no new shoot for this).
3. Record **one ~20-min long-form delivery** of the expanded talk → long-form proof + webinar/lead magnet.
4. Everything from the earlier playbook (one-sheet, emails, bureau + podcast outreach) proceeds unchanged.

---

## One-line summary

**Keep the topic finance (that's your moat and your fee); keep the tested "vibe banking" spine; expand 10 → 40 minutes by adding the failure-modes taxonomy, the Risk-Gate Stack, the maturity model, and a short frontier section from your new agents paper. Reuse all existing footage for the reel, and record exactly one ~20-minute long-form delivery to prove you can carry a full keynote. The agents paper is your depth, not a reason to leave finance.**
