# Senior Track — Activation Pack
### The weekend speaker kit, the bureau list (with links), the podcast list (with pitches), and the 2-week sprint

> This is the concrete execution layer for the Senior Track plan. Everything here is doable starting this weekend. No theory.

---

## 0. The one rule that sets the sequence

**Speaker bureaus amplify demand; they don't create it.** Most bureaus want speakers who are already established, command a fee, and have a track record. So the order is:

> **Build the kit (this weekend) → convert/secure 1–3 marquee or paid gigs → approach bureaus with proof of demand.**

You are closer to "established" than 95% of applicants (O'Reilly author, NVIDIA GTC / Fed Atlanta / AI4 / AI Summit NY stages, research credibility). You don't need to manufacture credibility — you need to *package* it so a bureau can sell you in 30 seconds. That's what the kit is.

## 1. The weekend build — your speaker kit (hour by hour)

Goal: by Sunday night you have a **link** (one URL) you can paste into any bureau form or podcast pitch. Five assets. Build them in this order.

### Asset 1 — The signature talk (the spine of everything) · ~2 hrs
You can't make a one-sheet or reel without first deciding the *one talk*. Lock it:

- **Title (own a question, not a topic):** e.g. *"Can You Trust an AI That Manages Money?"*
- **One-line promise:** what the audience walks away able to do/decide.
- **3 takeaways** (these go everywhere — one-sheet, pitches, podcast hooks).
- **The proprietary framework** you reveal on stage: *The Finance AI Risk-Gate Stack* and/or *The AI Trust Index for Financial Institutions*.

### Asset 2 — The speaker one-sheet (1-page PDF) · ~2 hrs
This is the single most-requested artifact by bureaus and event organizers. One page, scannable. Structure:

1. **Name + a positioning line** ("Quant + AI researcher · O'Reilly author · explaining the trust gap in financial AI")
2. **Headshot** (professional; reuse an existing good one)
3. **Signature talk title + the 3 takeaways**
4. **2–3 alternate talk titles** (gives organizers options)
5. **Bio (short, 80 words)** — peer-of-buyers register, not creator
6. **Marquee-logo strip** — NVIDIA GTC, Fed Atlanta, AI4, AI Summit NY, O'Reilly, Barclays-affiliation
7. **1–2 testimonial lines** (get these from past stage organizers this week if you don't have them)
8. **Contact + website + book link**

*Model it on:* the speaker pages of Lavin/Stern Strategy rosters (clean, idea-forward, logo-heavy). Search "[Ajay Agrawal Lavin]" or "[Azeem Azhar Stern Strategy]" to see the format.

### Asset 3 — The sizzle/demo reel (3–5 min) · ~3–4 hrs
Bureaus essentially **require** video. Two paths:
- **Best:** cut existing stage footage (GTC/AI4/etc.) into a 3-min reel — open with your sharpest 20-second line, intercut audience/stage shots, end with name + topic + contact.
- **If no footage:** record one clean 5–8 min delivery of your signature talk's core argument to camera (good light, good mic, plain background). A single strong studio clip beats no video.
- Upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine) + embed on your site.

### Asset 4 — The speaker page on your site · ~1–2 hrs
One page at htatsat.com/speaking that holds: the reel, the one-sheet (download button), talk titles, logos, testimonials, and a contact form. **This URL is what you paste everywhere.**

### Asset 5 — The pitch assets (text) · ~1 hr
- A 90-word bureau email (template in §3)
- A 120-word podcast pitch (template in §5)
- A 60-word bio + a 25-word bio (organizers ask for both)

**Weekend total: ~10–12 hours.** Outsource the reel edit and one-sheet design to a Fiverr/Upwork editor in parallel if you want it polished — but you can ship a credible v1 yourself.

## 3. What to send a bureau (the package + email)

**Package (one email):**
- Link to your speaker page (the one URL)
- One-sheet PDF attached
- Reel link
- 2-line note on demand proof (past stages, any fee history, inbound)

**Template email:**

> **Subject:** Representation — AI-in-finance keynote speaker (O'Reilly author, NVIDIA GTC / Fed)
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm a quantitative-finance and AI researcher and O'Reilly author. I speak on the trust gap in financial AI — why most AI in investment workflows fails in production, and the risk-gate architecture that fixes it. I've spoken at NVIDIA GTC, the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta, AI4, and the AI Summit NY.
>
> My signature keynote, *"Can You Trust an AI That Manages Money?"*, gives executives a concrete framework (the Finance AI Risk-Gate Stack) for adopting AI without blowing up on hidden failure modes.
>
> One-sheet and a short reel here: [URL]. I'd love to explore representation.
>
> Best, [name] · [book link]

Keep it under 120 words. Bureaus skim.

## 4. The podcast plan — tiers, links, and the pitch

Podcasts are your fastest, cheapest authority engine (and they produce the clips bureaus want). Go in tiers — land the bookable ones, use those clips to reach the big ones.

### Tier 1 — Bookable now (industry / enterprise / finance-AI)
High relevance, realistic to land, exactly your audience.
- **Eye on AI** (eye-on.ai) — industry analysis
- **The TWIML AI Podcast** (twimlai.com) — applied ML/AI
- **Practical AI** (practicalai.fm) — deployment-oriented
- **Me, Myself, and AI** (MIT Sloan Mgmt Review + BCG) — AI in the enterprise, executive audience
- **NVIDIA AI Podcast** (blogs.nvidia.com/ai-podcast) — you have a GTC tie-in
- **The AI Daily Brief** — business AI
- **AI in Financial Services Podcast / Emerj** — your exact niche
- **WealthTech Today** (Craig Iskowitz), **WealthStack** (WealthManagement.com) — wealth/advisor audience
- **CFA Institute** content/podcasts — credibility with the buy-side

### Tier 2 — Reach / aspirational (need the hook + a clip reel first)
- **No Priors** (Sarah Guo / Elad Gil) — business/investment AI
- **The Cognitive Revolution** (Nathan Labenz) — consequences of AI; loves a sharp technical-but-accessible thesis
- **Dwarkesh Podcast** — deep, research-y
- **Machine Learning Street Talk** — technically rigorous (your interpretability angle fits)
- **Hard Fork** (NYT) — culture/policy
- **Lex Fridman** — largest reach, long-form
- **Odd Lots** (Bloomberg), **Masters in Business** (Barry Ritholtz) — top finance shows
- **Exponential View** (Azeem Azhar) — exec audience; also a networking target

### The pitch formula
Hosts book **a sharp, contrarian, *specific* thesis** — not "I'd love to talk about AI." Lead with the disagreement.

**Template:**

> **Subject:** Pitch: why "human in the loop" doesn't make finance AI safe
>
> Hi [host],
>
> Big fan of [specific recent episode]. A thesis I think your audience would argue about: *most AI in finance fails in ways that look correct* — a confidently wrong investment memo passes human review precisely because the output looks clean. I work on this from the inside (quant + AI interpretability; O'Reilly author; GTC/Fed/AI4 stages), and I can explain why output monitoring misses it and what actually catches it (runtime risk gates / probing model internals).
>
> Concrete segments I could bring: the 7 silent failure modes of finance AI; why RAG over filings injects look-ahead bias; the "AI Trust Index" for financial institutions.
>
> Reel + bio: [URL]. Worth a conversation?
>
> [name]

Send **10–15/week**. Personalize the first line (reference a real episode) — that one line doubles reply rates.

## 6. The 2-week sprint (right after the weekend)

**Week 1**
- Mon: finalize kit; publish speaker page.
- Tue: email **Tier A bureaus** (Lavin, Stern Strategy, Leigh) with the §3 template.
- Wed: apply to **BigSpeak** + email **Leading Authorities**; list on SpeakerHub/Espeakers.
- Thu: go back to **AI-in-Finance Summit** organizer — ask to convert your slot to a **paid keynote**; same for any GTC/Fed/AI4 contacts.
- Fri: pitch **5 Tier-1 podcasts** (personalized).

**Week 2**
- Pitch **10 more podcasts** (mix Tier 1 + 2 reach shots).
- Apply to 4–6 conference call-for-speakers (QuantMinds, AI Summit NY, Finovate, CFA Society, ReWork).
- Start the **flagship report** outline (this is what turns "invited" into "paid" and makes bureaus chase you).
- Record/ship one sharp public essay built on the signature idea — the clip + the link that warms every pitch.

**Targets by end of sprint:** kit live · 6 bureaus contacted · 15 podcasts pitched · 5 conference applications in · 1 existing stage in conversion to paid · report outlined.
