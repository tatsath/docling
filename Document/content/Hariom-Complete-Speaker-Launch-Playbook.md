## 4. YOUR SIGNATURE TALK — COMPLETE BUILD

**Scope decision:** stay **finance-anchored**. The agents paper = a 4-minute "frontier" section inside the talk, not a broadening of topic. (A separate pure-AI talk can come later.)

**Reuse vs. record:** reuse all footage for the reel; your ~15-min Beyond the Black Box covers long-form proof, so **no new shoot needed now**. (Optional later: one clean 20-min studio delivery of the expanded talk as a webinar/lead magnet.)

**Title (two hooks, same talk):**
- Fintech/innovation crowds: **"Does Vibe Banking Actually Work?"** — *Can you trust an AI that manages money?*
- Board/asset-manager crowds: **"Can You Trust an AI That Manages Money?"** — *Beyond the demos: what works and fails in financial AI.*

**Keep (tested 10-min "vibe banking" spine):** the hook, your narrative arc, your best finance failure stories, your delivery, the lines that land.

**Create (expansion to ~40 min — depth, not a topic change):**
1. **Cold-open** (~2 min): a confidently-wrong AI output that passed human review.
2. **7 silent failure modes of finance AI** (~6 min): look-ahead leakage, table/number corruption, citation hallucination, eval blindness, tool-use overreach, data leakage, demo-to-production gap — one finance example each.
3. **The Risk-Gate Stack** (~6 min): retrieval → extraction → reasoning/eval → runtime probe → human approval. Your ownable diagram.
4. **Frontier section** (~4 min): your agents paper — how agents fail *internally* and what it means for trusting them with money/risk.
5. **Maturity model** (~4 min): Pilots → Tools → Workflows → Governed → Agentic-with-Gates. "Where is your firm?"
6. **Close + one CTA** (~2 min): the AI Trust Index idea + "the one thing to do Monday."

**Full 40-min flow:** cold-open → vibe-banking hook (kept) → the trust gap → 7 failure modes → Risk-Gate Stack → frontier (agents paper) → maturity model → close.

**Modular runtimes (one body of material):** 10 min = hook + 2 failure modes + close · 20 min = + Risk-Gate Stack · 40 min = full flow · half-day = full flow + hands-on exercise per gate.
